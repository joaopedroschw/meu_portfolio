Profile

01 / Biographical Summary

PhD Candidate in Contemporary Art at the College of Arts of the University of Coimbra, researching body, space, and performance. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Music – Performance from the University of Évora and a Master's degree in Music – Performance from the University of Aveiro. He currently serves as President of the Administrative Board of the Students' Theatre of the University of Coimbra (TEUC). He has experience in cultural project management, practice-based artistic research, community art, cultural associations, and musical performance.
