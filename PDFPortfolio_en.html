Artistic & Biographical Statement

Body, space, listening, and agency in collective performative practices.

João Pedro Schwingel Carada (Brazil, 1999) is a multidisciplinary artist, performer, musician, and researcher based in Coimbra. He is a PhD candidate in Contemporary Art at the College of Arts of the University of Coimbra, holding a Master's degree in Music (Performance — Oboe) from the University of Aveiro (2023) and a Bachelor's degree in Music from the University of Évora (2021).

His artistic activity unfolds across multiple fronts: he works both in authorial creation and co-creation of performances and sound installations, and in collaboration as a guest actor/performer in works by other creators and directors, as well as a sound designer/composer, executive producer, and mediator in community projects.

He is currently President of the Board of the Students' Theatre of the University of Coimbra (TEUC) and has taken part in projects with Ana Borralho & João Galante, Zé Paredes, Hugo Inácio, Vicente Baptista, Maria Quintelas, Íris Faria Vega, CITAC, Casa da Esquina, Anozero Ágora, and Orquestra Sem Fronteiras.

World of Interiors

Work and Artistic Direction by Ana Borralho & João Galante · Verão a Dois Tempos (N.I.B.)
My Participation: Performer (Artistic Residency and Public Presentation)
JULY 22, 2026 · LARGO DO ROMAL Residency: July 17 to 21, 2026 · Downtown Coimbra
About the Work & My Participation

WORLD OF INTERIORS is a performance-installation authored by artists Ana Borralho & João Galante. In this project, I participated as a guest performer, taking part in the preparatory artistic residency (July 17 to 21) and the public presentation at Largo do Romal, in Downtown Coimbra.

Faced with motionless and silent bodies arranged in public space, the audience is invited to abandon the role of mere observer, step closer, and build a relationship of intimacy. The performers whisper text fragments from the theatrical works of Rodrigo Garcia in a shared space, inviting those watching to find their own place to listen.

By exploring vulnerability and an intimate scale in urban space, the work questions the boundaries between the observer and the observed, transforming the artistic experience into a territory of encounter, silence, and sharing.

SLOW

Collective Creation by the Corpos Performáticos Group (College of Arts — UC)
My Role: Co-creator, Performer & Sound Designer
APRIL 28, 2026 · TAGV STAGE Abril Dança Coimbra Festival (CMC / TAGV)
About the Project & My Role

For two months, six artists from the Corpos Performáticos group of the PhD program in Contemporary Art at the College of Arts (UC), guided by Susana Chiocca, gathered to create SLOW as part of the Abril Dança Coimbra Festival.

In this project, I worked as a co-creator, stage performer, and sound designer / soundscape composer. Through dance, somatic methods, sound composition, and the subversion of Tai Chi, the collective proposed suspending time on the stage of Teatro Académico de Gil Vicente.

Attention and movement question the contemporary near-impossibility of stopping and contemplating: filming and observing snails, subverting the formal vocabulary of Tai Chi, serving tea, decolonizing the body and the word, making and unmaking so that desires stop being trampled by haste.

Café com Leite

Individual Creation · Corpos Performáticos Cycle (GPCA — College of Arts of UC)
My Role: Conception, Autoethnographic Text & Solo Performance (Authorial Work)
OCTOBER 24, 2025 · COIMBRA College of Arts of the University of Coimbra
Concept & Authorial Performative Action

café-com-leite (masc. noun / adj. — coffee with milk): 1. A brownish color similar to the color of coffee with milk. 4. One who is protected by indifference. Not understood enough to defend their position. Not naive enough not to participate in the action.

“When does someone become aware of their own body? And of the other's body? At what point do we build the bridge that connects us? And the sea that separates us? 130 km away there is a sense of belonging. Inside the door: affection. Outside the door: indifference.”

An authorial solo performance stemming from an autoethnographic investigation into racial identity, miscegenation, and displacement, anchored in my childhood memories in southern Brazil. The action unfolds in three moments: it begins as a relational device of hospitality, serving coffee with milk to passersby; shifts into an intimate ritual, bathing my own body in coffee with milk while reading memories from my diary; and concludes with washing my body with soap and water.

Pássaros Mortos Ainda Fazem Sombra

TEUC Collective Creation · Directed by Vicente Baptista
My Role: Actor, Co-creator (Text/Set/Costumes) & Sound Designer
MAY 21 TO 31, 2025 · 7 PERFORMANCES TEUC's Teatro de Bolso (Coimbra)
About the Show & My Role

“There are nights that never end. Nights that drag on like roots, sink into the body, darken thought. Here, time is folded. The horizon is not a line, it is absence. And even so, something insists on dancing.”

Like birds that no longer know how to migrate, six bodies float between what was and what perhaps still has no name. In this territory of delirium and wind, reality dissolves in time, and every step can be a dream, an end, a beginning.

A collective creation by TEUC directed by Vicente Baptista. In this project, I worked simultaneously as a stage actor, co-creator of the dramaturgy, set design, and costumes, and designer of the original soundscape and sound art, composing the electroacoustic textures and sonic environments that structure the atmosphere of the play.

O Não-Lugar Que Habito

Co-creation with Maria Vitória do Valle 2024–2025 · Casa da Esquina & TAGV
My Role: Co-creator, Soundscape & Performance

A performative and installation experience co-created and presented as a duo with Maria Vitória do Valle, exploring the notions of the immateriality of “home” and “saudade” within the migratory experience.

While listening through headphones to a soundscape composed of interviews and memories, the audience is invited to collectively paint a canvas and build a clothesline of memories.

De Onde Vem? Canções Para Conversar e Criar

Orquestra Sem Fronteiras (OSF) · 2nd Edition of the Terra a Terra Program
My Role: Project Conception, Pedagogical Coordination & Artistic Mediation
2022 · CASTELO BRANCO Partnership: “Nós com os Outros” Project (Amato Lusitano)
Conception & Community Work

De Onde Vem? Canções Para Conversar e Criar was a community art and musical co-creation project conceived, coordinated, and facilitated by me within the 2nd edition of the Terra a Terra program by Orquestra Sem Fronteiras (OSF).

Developed in Castelo Branco with young immigrants (aged 6 to 26) participating in the project “Nós com os Outros” (Amato Lusitano Association), the project used popular song as a territory for encounter, musical training, and collective expression.

Throughout sessions of body percussion, voice, lyric writing, and shared listening, artistic practice served as a tool for social inclusion, identity affirmation, and intercultural dialogue.

11.A / Authorial Performance in Interactive Installation (Master's — UA)

Let Me Sleep Now

University of Aveiro (Superv. Paulo Maria Rodrigues) 2023 · Aveiro
My Role: Conception, Sound Installation, Oboe & Performance

Authorial creation developed during my Master's in Music at the University of Aveiro. It stems from the deconstruction of War Requiem (Britten), Rite de la Lune (E. Michaël), and Imaginary Landscape No. 1 (John Cage), juxtaposing pages of 20th-century Portuguese colonial newspapers with contemporary newspapers suspended on clotheslines.

Using a keyboard at the entrance, the audience altered in real time both the soundscape and my physical and musical actions on the oboe up to the installation's point of collapse.

Technical Credits & Dissertation DECA-UA · 2023

Conception, Installation, and Performance (Oboe/Body): João Pedro Carada | Dissertation: “Performance em Instalação: possíveis diálogos entre intérprete, público e espaço”

11.B / Authorial Performance in Sound and Participatory Installation

Onde Se Faz o Espaço: Uma Metamorfose

Liquidâmbar (Coimbra) · Ex Aequo FM Event November 19, 2022 · Coimbra
My Role: Conception, Sound Cartography & Solo Oboe

First installation performance of my Master's research cycle. It places Benjamin Britten's Six Metamorphoses after Ovid, Op. 49 for solo oboe in dialogue with a sound cartography of Coimbra recorded by me, reflecting on metamorphosis, immigration, and queer memory.

Blindfolded and with ropes tied to my body and stretched out to the audience's chairs, I performed Britten from memory while the audience drew what they heard and hung their sheets on the lines uniting us.
